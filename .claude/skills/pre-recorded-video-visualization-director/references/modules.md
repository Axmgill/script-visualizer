# Conditional Modules

Apply each module whenever its trigger condition matches the user's inputs. Modules stack: a vertical, fast-cut, branded TikTok edit applies all three relevant modules at once.

## Branding Module

Trigger: brand guidelines are provided. Apply them strictly.

Check:

- logo placement
- logo animation
- color palette
- typography
- tone of voice
- safe zones
- prohibited imagery
- partner/sponsor mentions
- legal/disclaimer text
- platform-specific UI obstruction

If no brand guidelines are provided, use neutral clean branding and mark it as assumed.

## Vertical Video Module

Trigger: the video is for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or similar platforms. Automatically account for:

- 9:16 framing
- top UI safe zone
- bottom caption/UI safe zone
- side margins
- thumb-stopping hook in first 1–2 seconds
- large readable text
- mobile-first composition
- vertical subject placement
- avoiding important details near edges

Working guideline when the platform gives no exact numbers: keep critical text and faces within roughly the middle 60–70% of the frame height, clear of the top ~10% and bottom ~20–25%, with ~5–8% side margins. Label specific pixel values as assumptions and verify against the platform's current UI.

## Documentary / Authenticity Module

Trigger: the user wants realism. Apply:

- handheld camera feel
- natural light
- imperfect framing
- real environments
- young people in natural settings
- subtle movement
- authentic reactions
- minimal artificial polish
- documentary lens language
- 35mm-style framing when requested
- Arri Amira-like texture when requested
- avoid overly staged stock behavior

## Fast-Cut / High-Energy Module

Trigger: the user wants energetic editing. Apply:

- word-level emphasis
- punch-ins
- beat-synced cuts
- kinetic captions
- quick visual resets
- pattern interrupts every few seconds
- dynamic motion graphics
- rhythmic pauses
- contrast between fast and slow moments

## AI Footage Module

Trigger: AI-generated footage is involved. Plan for:

- prompt-ready visual descriptions
- consistent character/style notes
- camera movement prompts
- lens and lighting prompts
- negative prompts if needed
- avoiding fake-looking hands/faces
- keeping motion believable
- matching generated clips to edit rhythm
- generating alternate takes for difficult shots
- using AI footage only where it supports the pre-recorded voiceover
